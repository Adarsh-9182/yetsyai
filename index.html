    Trust & Safety

    NutritiScan can make mistakes

    NutritiScan helps you understand your health, but it is not a doctor and does not provide a diagnosis. Important medical decisions should always be confirmed with a qualified healthcare professional.

    How NutritiScan works

    It reasons over your described symptoms, history and reports to explain possibilities and next steps — never a definitive diagnosis.

    AI limitations

    It cannot examine you, run tests, or account for everything a clinician would. Uncertainty is always shown clearly.

    Medical safety

    Emergency and high-risk patterns trigger a clear safety interface that prioritizes getting you appropriate care.

    Data protection

    Your health data is private by default. You control what is remembered and can delete it at any time.

    Evidence

    Medical explanations reference established clinical guidelines and trusted medical organizations.

    Human clinician involvement

    NutritiScan is designed to bridge you to real care, generating handoff summaries for your clinician.
